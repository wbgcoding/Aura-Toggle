The only thing asked in return is that the copyright line and this licence stay with the

WHAT IT DOES TO YOUR MAINBOARD

Aura Toggle only sends volatile lighting commands. It never writes to the controller's
flash memory, so your BIOS lighting settings stay untouched and a restart brings them back.
It needs no administrator rights and installs no driver and no background service.

That said, this is software talking directly to hardware, and it comes with no warranty of
any kind. If you do not accept that, do not install it.
