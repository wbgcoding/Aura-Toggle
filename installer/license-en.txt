PRIVACY AND NETWORK ACCESS

The application collects no data, needs no account, and carries no advertising or tracking.
Your settings, presets and the remembered lighting state stay in your own user profile on
this PC and are never sent anywhere. Aura Toggle itself makes no network connection at all.

If this PC lacks the Microsoft .NET 10 Desktop Runtime the application needs to run, this
setup offers to download it from Microsoft - the one network access anywhere in this package,
only with your consent, its digital signature verified before installation.


EFFECT ON YOUR MAINBOARD

Aura Toggle talks to your mainboard's lighting controller with volatile commands only - it
never writes to the controller's flash memory. Every change is lost on restart, and the
lighting set in your BIOS/UEFI comes back automatically. Running it needs no administrator
rights, no driver and no background service; installing it for all users asks for
administrator rights once, for that installation alone.
